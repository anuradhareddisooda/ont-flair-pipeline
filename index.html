Pipeline steps

      

        
01  cat / cp            Merge FASTQ files for multi-lane samples

        
02  flair align         Map ONT reads to genome

        
03  flair correct       Correct splice junctions using GTF

        
04  flair collapse      Collapse reads into isoform models

        
05  flair combine       Merge isoforms across all samples

        
06  flair quantify      Quantify isoform expression

        
07  predictProductivity  Predict productive vs NMD isoforms

        
08  plot_isoform_usage   Plot isoform usage for your gene
